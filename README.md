# 🌸 Happy Birthday, Ammanda! 🌸✨

A luxurious, ultra-girly, interactive birthday website crafted specially for **Ammanda**.

## ✨ Features Included:
- **Royal Entrance & Floating Balloons**: Pastel balloons spelling "A M M A N D A" floating in the sky. Tap any balloon to pop it with realistic sound effects and discover secret compliments!
- **Royal Wax-Sealed Birthday Letter**: An authentic wax-sealed envelope ("A") that breaks open to reveal a heartwarming, customized letter.
- **Interactive 3D Cake & Candle Blowing**: Multi-tiered pastel cake with real flickering candle flames. Click "Blow Out the Candles" to trigger wind whoosh sounds, smoke puffs, celebratory fanfare, and screen-wide fireworks & confetti!
- **Constellation Wish Jar**: Make a secret wish and seal it in Ammanda's glowing jar (saved in browser memory).
- **3D Wrapped Mystery Gift Box**: Untie the satin ribbon and pop off the lid to reveal 6 VIP Birthday Perk Coupons (Princess Privilege, Spa Day, Cafe Spree, Shopping Hype, Cozy Movie Night, and Bestie Hug Hotline).
- **10 Reasons We Adore Ammanda**: 10 3D interactive flip-cards celebrating Ammanda's kindness, humor, iconic style, and radiant laughter.
- **The Queen's Magic Mirror (🪞)**: Shimmering royal mirror that reveals customized, uplifting fortunes and compliments on every tap.
- **Bakery Studio (Cupcake Designer)**: Mix and match sponge flavors, frosting swirls, and royal toppers (crown, strawberry, bow, cherry, sparkler) to create and serve a custom birthday treat.
- **Fairy Light Corkboard Guestbook**: Pre-pinned with sweet messages, plus an interactive form to write custom notes with pastel colors and signature stickers (saved in LocalStorage).
- **Polaroid Scrapbook Gallery**: Aesthetic polaroids hung with washi tape, with an instant photo uploader to add real memories.
- **Synthesized Music Box Player**: Uses Web Audio API (zero external MP3 dependencies) to play "Happy Birthday", "Pastel Dream Lullaby", and "Sparkling Celebration Chimes" with an animated spinning vinyl disc.
- **Sparkle Magic Wand Cursor Trail & Party Popper**: Trailing stars and hearts behind the cursor, and a party cannon button for glitter showers!

## 🚀 How to Open & View:
Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox!
Or run:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
and visit `http://localhost:8000`.
